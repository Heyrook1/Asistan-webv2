# Asistan Health Premium Brand Reveal Reel

## Creative Direction

The reel presents the existing Asistan logo as a compact visual story:

- The three speed lines introduce flow and operational momentum.
- The human dot and the stylized `A` assemble into the smart assistant mark.
- The product promise is presented through three restrained feature cards.
- `Asistan Rezervasyon` is announced as an upcoming product.
- The reel closes with the Asistan Health identity, demo CTA, and KKTC URL.
